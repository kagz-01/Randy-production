# Randy Production: Project Cost Breakdown

**Proposed fixed development fee:** **KES 127,500**

**Currency:** Kenyan shillings

**Pricing basis:** Fixed value for the focused scope described below—not an
hourly estimate.

## Project investment

This proposal allocates KES 127,500 to a focused, launch-ready foundation for
Randy Production: a clear public enquiry journey, essential client and
operations workflows, reliable data and access controls, and a prepared
deployment handover.

The value is in connecting those parts into one usable service—not simply
delivering a collection of pages. A prospective client can understand the
offer and submit an enquiry; the team can review and coordinate agreed work;
and clients can access the information and actions included in this phase.

## What makes this investment worthwhile

- **A joined-up client journey:** public discovery and enquiry lead into the
  team's review process instead of ending in disconnected pages or messages.
- **Useful workspaces:** clients and the internal team receive interfaces
  shaped around their different responsibilities.
- **A stronger foundation:** data, authentication, and access rules support
  the workflows behind the screens.
- **Designed for real use:** responsive layouts and clear interaction states
  help people use the service on both phones and desktop.
- **A practical handover:** deployment notes and operating guidance make the
  work easier to manage after delivery.

The fee is based on the agreed deliverables and their role in the product. It
does not promise a specific level of revenue, customer growth, or time saved;
those outcomes depend on business operations and customer adoption.

## Scope and boundaries

This proposal covers the focused set of deliverables described in this
document. Before work starts, the parties should agree the exact pages,
workflows, acceptance criteria, and delivery schedule.

Unless expressly added to the signed scope, this fee excludes advanced finance
and receipt workflows, extensive crew/gear/vendor/merchandise management,
complex reporting, native mobile apps, paid third-party integrations,
independent security testing, ongoing maintenance, and provider subscription
or usage charges. Any work outside the agreed scope requires a separate
estimate and written approval.

Hosting, database, domain, and email-provider charges are paid separately to
the relevant providers. The proposed deployment setup is Vercel for
application hosting, Supabase PostgreSQL for the database, Cloudflare for DNS,
and Resend for transactional email, subject to the final agreed configuration.
These operating costs are not included in the KES 127,500 development fee.

VAT, if legally chargeable, is additional. The final contract should state the
applicable tax treatment, delivery dates, payment schedule, defect-correction
period, and the precise acceptance criteria.
